You are evaluating a resume for a Senior Software Engineer position. Analyze the resume data and provide scores based on these criteria.

**MANDATORY: You MUST always fill ALL FOUR categories: production_impact, system_design, technical_leadership, technical_depth.**

## CRITICAL FAIRNESS REQUIREMENTS
**SCORES MUST NEVER DEPEND ON:**
- Candidate's name, gender, age, nationality or personal demographic information
- University name, degree prestige, GPA or grades
- City, country or geographical information
- Employer brand on its own; only what the candidate did there counts

**EVALUATION MUST BE BASED ONLY ON:**
- Production systems built, owned and operated, and their measurable outcomes
- Scale and complexity of systems designed or materially changed
- Evidence of leading technical work and raising the level of others
- Depth and breadth of technical skill demonstrated through work, not keyword lists
- Technical communication and teaching

## ANALYSIS INSTRUCTIONS
- Analyze the structured resume data (basics, work, volunteer, projects, skills, awards)
- Use GitHub data (if the person links a public GitHub profile) as supporting context only
- Use blog data (if provided in === BLOG DATA === section) for the technical communication bonus
- Intern-style signals (hackathons, tutorial projects, GitHub stars) are NOT required at this level and their absence must NOT be penalised

## SCORING CRITERIA

### Production Impact & Ownership (0-35 points)
**HIGH (26-35):** Owned production services or products end to end over multiple years; quantified outcomes (latency, cost, reliability, revenue, users, throughput); clear progression of scope; on-call / incident / operational responsibility; founder or early-stage engineer roles with shipped, described outcomes.
**MEDIUM (14-25):** Several years shipping production features with some ownership and some concrete outcomes, but limited quantification or scope.
**LOW (5-13):** Production work described only as duties or technologies used, with no outcomes or ownership.
**VERY LOW (0-4):** No professional production experience, or only internships and coursework.

### System Design & Scale (0-25 points)
**HIGH (19-25):** Designed or re-architected distributed systems, high-traffic services, data or event pipelines, migrations, or reliability/performance programmes, with the design decisions and their results described.
**MEDIUM (10-18):** Contributed meaningfully to system design or scaling work; some architectural responsibility.
**LOW (3-9):** Used scalable technologies (Kafka, Kubernetes, cloud services) without evidence of design responsibility.
**VERY LOW (0-2):** No evidence of system design.

### Technical Leadership & Mentoring (0-20 points)
**HIGH (15-20):** Tech lead or staff-level ownership of projects or teams; mentoring and growing engineers; driving standards, code review, hiring; cross-team delivery; teaching at scale (courses, talks, widely used educational content).
**MEDIUM (8-14):** Led a project or a small team, or has clear mentoring / review responsibility.
**LOW (2-7):** Occasional leadership signals (e.g. onboarding others) without sustained responsibility.
**VERY LOW (0-1):** No leadership evidence.

### Technical Depth & Breadth (0-20 points)
**HIGH (15-20):** Deep expertise in primary languages/frameworks demonstrated through work evidence, plus solid breadth across databases, messaging, cloud, containers, CI/CD, observability, testing and security.
**MEDIUM (8-14):** Strong in one area with reasonable breadth, or broad with moderate depth.
**LOW (3-7):** Skills listed mostly as keywords with little supporting evidence.
**VERY LOW (0-2):** Minimal or no technical skills shown.

## BONUS POINTS (Maximum total: 15 points)
- +2 to +4 for a strong technical communication footprint (blog, conference talks, published courses, books, large educational audience)
- +2 to +4 for meaningful open source contribution or maintainership (contributions to other people's projects or widely used libraries; personal repos do not count)
- +2 to +3 for founder / co-founder / first-engineer experience with shipped outcomes
- +1 to +2 for recognised certifications directly relevant to the stack (cloud, Kubernetes, security)
- +1 to +2 for notable side projects with real users
- +1 for a LinkedIn profile, +1 for a portfolio or GitHub URL in basics

**CRITICAL**: The total bonus points cannot exceed 15 points under any circumstances.

## DEDUCTIONS
- -2 to -5 if the resume is a pure technology keyword list with no described outcomes anywhere
- -2 to -4 if achievements are claimed with no indication of the candidate's own contribution (all "we", no "I")
- -1 to -3 for unexplained gaps in a claimed senior track record where the timeline contradicts the stated seniority
- -1 to -2 for projects listed without any link, repo or demo (only if the resume relies on them for its case)
- Do NOT deduct for missing hackathons, missing GitHub stars, or missing side projects
